# Supply Chain Research Hypotheses

## Purpose

The following hypotheses define the principal research questions for the supply chain analysis.

They are designed to move the investigation beyond descriptive reporting and toward measurable relationships between operational characteristics, delivery performance, and freight cost.

Each hypothesis will be evaluated using an analytical method appropriate to the structure and quality of the underlying data.

---

## H1 — Shipment Mode

**Research question:**  
Does transportation mode affect delivery delay?

**Analytical focus:**  
Compare delivery-delay outcomes across shipment modes and determine whether observed differences are statistically supported.

---

## H2 — Fulfillment Strategy

**Research question:**  
Does fulfillment strategy affect delivery performance?

**Comparison:**  
- Direct Drop
- RDC

**Analytical focus:**  
Determine whether delivery outcomes differ between the two fulfillment approaches.

---

## H3 — Vendor Performance

**Research question:**  
Do delivery outcomes differ significantly across major vendors?

**Analytical focus:**  
Evaluate vendor-level differences while considering shipment volume and the credibility of observed performance differences.

---

## H4 — Geography

**Research question:**  
Does destination country or region have a significant relationship with delivery delay?

**Analytical focus:**  
Investigate whether geographic destination is associated with differences in delivery performance.

---

## H5 — Product Group

**Research question:**  
Does product category influence delivery performance?

**Analytical focus:**  
Determine whether delivery outcomes vary across product groups.

---

## H6 — Shipment Weight

**Research question:**  
Is heavier freight associated with higher transportation costs?

**Analytical focus:**  
Evaluate the relationship between shipment weight and freight cost.

---

## H7 — Shipment Value

**Research question:**  
Is commodity value associated with freight cost?

**Analytical focus:**  
Investigate whether shipment value is related to transportation expenditure.

---

## H8 — Freight Efficiency

**Research question:**  
Does freight cost per kilogram differ significantly across shipment modes?

**Analytical focus:**  
Compare freight efficiency across transportation methods using freight cost relative to shipment weight.

---

## H9 — Commercial Process

**Research question:**  
Does the time between procurement milestones influence delivery performance?

**Analytical focus:**  
Investigate whether the duration between relevant commercial or procurement events is associated with subsequent delivery outcomes.

---

## H10 — Manufacturing Origin

**Research question:**  
Are manufacturing sites associated with different delivery lead times?

**Analytical focus:**  
Compare delivery lead times across manufacturing origins and assess whether observed differences are supported by the data.

---

## Hypothesis Framework

The hypotheses collectively cover four major analytical dimensions:

| Dimension | Hypotheses | Primary focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Delivery Performance** | H1–H5 | Shipment mode, fulfillment, vendors, geography, and product group |
| **Freight Economics** | H6–H8 | Weight, shipment value, and freight efficiency |
| **Commercial Process** | H9 | Procurement milestone timing |
| **Manufacturing Origin** | H10 | Manufacturing-site differences |

These hypotheses establish the analytical questions that will guide subsequent exploratory analysis, statistical testing, and modeling.
